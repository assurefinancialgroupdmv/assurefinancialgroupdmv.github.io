Some People Choose a Career. Others Are Called to One.

Born in Washington, D.C. and rooted in Maryland, Adrienne Brown lost her mother to endometrial cancer with no life insurance in place. There was no policy and no will, and the painful silence that followed became her mission: making sure no family has to navigate loss and financial hardship at the same time.

Before earning her license in March 2025, Adrienne built a richly diverse career: administrative assistant, phlebotomist, seasonal videographer for a semi-pro basketball team, the food industry, and most notably founder and owner of Pro Clean Xtreme, her own commercial cleaning company. That entrepreneurial background gives her a perspective most insurance professionals simply don't have. Today she is an independent agent and registered representative, which means she works for her clients, not for a single company, shopping multiple top-rated carriers to find the best coverage at the most competitive rate.
